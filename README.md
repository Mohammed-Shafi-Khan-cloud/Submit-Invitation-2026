# Professionals Summit 2026 — Official Digital Invitation & Pass Microsite

**Theme:** *“Thriving in a Changing World: Career, Life & Society”*  
**Date:** Sunday, 13 December 2026 • 9:00 AM – 5:30 PM IST  
**Venue:** FTCCI (Federation of Telangana Chambers of Commerce and Industry), Red Hills, Lakdikapul, Hyderabad

## Key Features
- **Botanical Emerald Editorial Design:** Warm alabaster stationery palette paired with deep forest emerald (`#0B3B2E`), vibrant emerald (`#146C53`), and heritage gold (`#B8860B`).
- **Personalized Tier Invitation Links:** Automatically adapts the top invitation banner and pass selection using URL parameters.
- **Live Countdown & Calendar Export:** Real-time countdown to December 13, 2026 (IST) with downloadable `.ics` (Apple/Outlook) and Google Calendar integration.
- **Interactive 5 Pillars & 8-Session Schedule:** Click any pillar card to filter the full-day summit agenda.
- **Instant Digital Entry Pass Generator:** Generates a personalized 2026 Entry Pass (`PSF26-HYD-XXXX`) with a verification QR code and one-click **Download Pass (PNG)** export.

## Tier-Specific Invitation Links
Append any of the following query parameters to your live GitHub Pages URL when sharing on WhatsApp or email:
- **VIP Guests:** `https://<your-username>.github.io/professionals-summit-2026/?tier=vip`
- **Speakers & Panelists:** `https://<your-username>.github.io/professionals-summit-2026/?tier=speaker`
- **General Delegates:** `https://<your-username>.github.io/professionals-summit-2026/?tier=delegate`

---

## Updating Confirmed Speakers
All speaker cards are stored in the `SPEAKERS_DATA` array inside `index.html`. Simply update the `name`, `role`, `topic`, and `bio` fields as new speaker waves are announced.
